# Deities and Pantheons
## Generic believes
Temples exist across Solemia. Clerics, paladins, and believers are common.

Faith is woven into society but interpreted differently depending on region. Most people do not question the gods.
Some do.

## Deities
The main believe in this world is: Ying & Yang, Life & Death, Up & Down, Cold & Warm. 
Many have different interpretations of the gods. Sol, Mother Nature, Everlight, **the First Flame**. Luna, Necros, **the Endless Night**.

### The First Flame
Usually associated with:
* Life
* Growth
* Passion
* Light

|Province, Region|Local name|Title|Description|
